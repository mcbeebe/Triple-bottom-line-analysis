One conversation: tap or type

A single chat thread. The guided steps are quick-reply buttons inside the conversation, so there is one question and one thing to do at a time. Typing a sentence works at any point and jumps ahead: the analyst summarizes what it understood, asks the one question that changes the cost, and shows the answer.
